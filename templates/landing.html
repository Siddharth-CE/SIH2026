Statutory Procurement System Integration Notice

GovPilot is designed as an innovation, testing, and pre-qualification layer. It does not bypass or replace formal government procurement portals (e.g. GeM, CPPP, State e-Procurement Systems). Successful pilots produce an audited Scale-Up Pack with validated performance specifications and risk assessments to accelerate authorized statutory procurement under Rule 149 / Open Innovation clauses.
