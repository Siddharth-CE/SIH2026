Multi-step wizard to formulate quantifiable public sector innovation challenges.

AI Challenge Assistant

Describe your operational problem in plain language to generate a complete 8-step outcome blueprint.

Natural Language to Blueprint
1. Problem
2. Current State
3. Desired Outcome
4. Pilot Scope
5. Data & APIs
6. Constraints
7. Evaluation
8. Review & Publish

Step 1 — Problem Definition

Define the core public operational bottleneck without specifying solutions.

Step 2 — Current State & Baseline Metrics

Establish benchmark metrics to evaluate future pilot improvements.

Step 3 — Desired Outcome & Target Improvement

State what success looks like in measurable terms.

Step 4 — Controlled Pilot Scope & Boundaries

Bound the test to contain operational risk while testing under real conditions.

Step 5 — Data & API Sandboxes

Specify what telemetry, APIs, and sandbox records the department will provide.

Step 6 — Technical Constraints & Security Protocols

Define compliance and infrastructure guardrails.

Step 7 — Evaluation Criteria & Success Thresholds

Establish how startup proposals and pilot results will be verified.

Step 8 — Blueprint Review & Publication

Review the finalized outcome challenge before releasing to the startup ecosystem.
