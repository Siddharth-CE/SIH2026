Stage 1: Government Has an Operational Problem Initial Bottleneck

Urban Public Bus Delays & Commuter Uncertainty

The Urban Transport Department manages over 450 municipal buses. Due to peak hour traffic variability, buses frequently experience severe vehicle bunching (multiple buses on the same route arriving at once followed by a 30-minute void). Commuters experience average wait times of 16.4 minutes and lack accurate real-time arrival forecasts.

Why Traditional Procurement Fails Here:
Standard procurement tender documents prescribe rigid hardware specifications rather than outcome metrics. Commercial off-the-shelf systems cost millions and require lengthy tender cycles with no guaranteed performance reduction in passenger wait times.
Baseline Metrics
• Average Wait Time: 16.4 min
• Schedule Adherence: 61.2%
• Commuter ETA Accuracy: 58.0%
• Telemetry Availability: 96.5%
